# VR Player Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

VR Player does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- The app works entirely offline and makes no network connections.
- The videos you open are read directly from your own device and are never uploaded.
- Your preferences (volume, video format, eye selection) and the playback position for each video are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- The app contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party tracking.

If you have questions, please open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
